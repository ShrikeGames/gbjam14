# gbjam14
Entry for GMJam14: Gameboy Styled Game

Game Jam Information: https://itch.io/jam/gbjam-14

Secondary Theme: OLD GOLD

## Day 1:

# Game Ideas:
- A Motherload-type game: Digging through the earth for minerals (gold, fossils and old stuff too), bring back to surface, sell, upgrade, repeat
- A Pawn-shop Simulator: Buy and sell to try and make profit, include papers-please style narrative mechanics?
- Dig Dug style game?
- Ancient Dragon has to hoard gold and protect it from adventurers, or vice versa play as the adventurers
- 2D Platformer collect-a-thon in the style of N+
- Nail N Scale style puzzle platformer but destroying blocks also reveals the gold to collect not just to progress
  - Mix of puzzle, platforming, and action
  - 
- Lemmings Style Game with the goal to lead them to gold to mine AND have to return back home with it alive
  - Procedural animation on the creatures/"lemmings"
  - Level selection
  - Each level introduces new concepts/problems to overcome
  - They can swing pickaxes to dig, collect the gold and carry it over their heads back (thus making them taller too?)
  - Player places different objective types and the "lemmings" go there based on their "needs" like Sims AI
  - Different types of "lemmings" have different "needs" priorities
  - Biggest problem is controls/screen size.

- Combination of above:
  - Fly your mobile fortress, press A to release a lemming, If they get back to you they get back in
  - Your mobile fortress is what camera is focused on, and it's larger so needs more space to get through
  - Lemmings collect the ancient artifacts and gold and bring back to you
  - Can accidently crush your lemmings if you land on them?
  - SELECT button to switch between lemmings types to release
  - Procedural animation for the lemmings (EG: rainworld)

A combination of motherload and lemmings. Your character is a somewhat large mobile fortress/machine you control with the dpad/arrow keys. It can dig straight down using a drill but not to the sides. Then you can use SELECT to change between different types of lemmings and N to release one to the left, or A to release to the right of your character. They can then dig or do whatever ability they have to make them unique, pick up artifacts/gold/minerals and if they bring it back to your character then you get it. Camera can just follow your character simplifying that process of having a massive map. Bring the loot back to the surface to be given upgrade options to buy, new lemmings to recruit, or something like that.
You're digging up old remains of a post-apocalyptic world.
Since the world has effectively ended we've gone back to the old gold-standard for currency.
